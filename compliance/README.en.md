# Deferred compliance boundary

[한국어](README.md) · [简体中文](README.zh-CN.md) · [English](README.en.md)

Initial jurisdictions: United States (federal; state undecided) and United Arab Emirates, Abu Dhabi (ADGM versus mainland undecided).

Privacy settlement is implemented first. Tax-rate calculation, AI legal preparation, payroll deductions, treaty analysis, real KYB provider calls and statutory filing are **not implemented** in this core.

Future flow: select B2B / employee / contractor → resolve jurisdictions and dated official rules → calculate with deterministic rules → prepare cited AI drafts → attach an encrypted document package and its commitment to a receipt. Do not put legal names, beneficial-owner documents, salaries, or raw tax documents on-chain.

The KYB SDK checks signatures from explicitly trusted issuers, subject binding, expiry and supplied revocations. It does not establish identity by itself and is not a circuit-enforced admission policy. A private credential eligibility circuit can be added as a separately versioned pool.
